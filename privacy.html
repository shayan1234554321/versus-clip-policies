Privacy Policy

Versus Clip — Version 1.0.0  ·  Effective: May 3, 2026

Versus Clip ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Advertising ID — The Google Mobile Ads SDK accesses your device's advertising ID to serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob. We do not directly collect or store this ID in our app.
  • Device Information — The Google Mobile Ads SDK may receive basic device information (such as device type, OS version, and app version) as part of normal ad delivery. Our app does not actively collect or store this data beyond what the SDK requires for advertising purposes.
  • Photo Library — We request access to your photo library when you choose to select a profile picture. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Local App Data — Game state, player profiles, and settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not transmitted to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To serve and optimize advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To enable in-app features such as profile picture selection and game progress tracking
  • To improve app stability and performance

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Used for displaying advertisements. Google's privacy policy governs its data practices. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any other analytics services.
Note on Ad Personalization: We may request non-personalized ads when serving advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's advertising ID settings (Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Opt Out).

4. Data Storage & Security

Most data (player profiles, game progress, settings) is stored locally on your device and is protected by your device's operating system. We do not knowingly collect or store sensitive personal information from children under 13.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • As required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Versus Clip or its users

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of certain data collection or advertising uses

To exercise your rights, you can delete the app or clear app data from your device settings, which will remove locally stored information.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. We do not maintain external servers that retain your personal data.

8. Children's Privacy

Versus Clip is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
