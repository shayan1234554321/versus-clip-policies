Child Safety Policy

Versus Clip — Version 1.0.0  ·  Effective: May 5, 2026

Versus Clip is committed to ensuring the safety and well-being of children. This policy outlines our standards and practices to prevent child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) within our mobile application.

1. Our Commitment

Versus Clip has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation. We are dedicated to creating a safe environment for all users, particularly children and adolescents. Our app is not directed to children under 13, and we take proactive measures to prevent the sexual exploitation of minors.

2. Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

We maintain the following standards to protect children from sexual abuse and exploitation:

  • Strict prohibition of any content depicting, representing, or implying the sexual exploitation of children
  • Prohibition of any user-generated content that sexualizes minors in any way
  • No facilitation of any form of child prostitution or sex trafficking
  • Prohibition of grooming behaviors, luring, or any attempt to establish inappropriate relationships with minors
  • No collection of personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent

3. User Safety Features

  • Age Restriction: Versus Clip is intended for users 13 years of age or older. Users under 13 require parental or guardian consent.
  • Content Moderation: Any content reported or flagged as potentially involving the exploitation of minors will be reviewed and removed immediately.
  • Blocking & Reporting: Users can block and report other users who engage in inappropriate behavior.
  • No Direct Messaging: The app does not include direct messaging features between users, reducing opportunities for grooming.

4. Reporting Mechanism

If you encounter any content or behavior that you believe constitutes child sexual abuse or exploitation, please report it immediately:

  • Email: kshayan091@gmail.com
  • Google Play Store: Use the "Flag a Concern" option in the Google Play Store listing
Emergency: If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at missingkids.org or call 1-800-843-5678.

5. Compliance with Applicable Laws

Versus Clip complies with all applicable laws regarding child protection, including but not limited to:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Child Sexual Abuse Prevention requirements under Google Play Developer Program Policies
  • Applicable international laws regarding the protection of minors from sexual exploitation

6. Transparency & Accountability

We are committed to transparency regarding our child safety practices. This policy is publicly available and will be updated as needed to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws.
